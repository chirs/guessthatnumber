## Guess That Number! 

The dumbest guessing game I can imagine.


#### History

Invented on an overnight bus from Delhi to Dharamsala, late 2008 or
early 2009, with Miryam and Setiawan. The bus was bumpy and unpleasant
and took all night. Miryam and I made up the game together to get
through it. You say how many numbers there are. One of us holds one
of them, the other guesses, yes or no. We played for hours. Neither of us remembers how it ended. The Dalai
Lama wasn't the plan. He just showed up.

The road from that bus is in the background.


#### Rules

1. Pick how many numbers are in play
2. Guess a number
3. If wrong, guess again
4. Repeat until correct
5. There are no hints
6. There is no strategy
7. If you don't give up, you will eventually win.
